# W.O.D.U
World Of Developers Union

Hello dear developers community,
I am writing to you with the scope of formally creating the World Of Developers Union(W.O.D.U.) community. I will explain in the first section the reasoning, motivation and drive to form this community while in the second section the W.O.D.U. manifesto will be presented.
Let us establish also some common ground first. I consider developers to be everyone who contributes to the development of a software product in the information technology industry, including software developers, software architects, software testers, DevOps engineers, data engineers, technical writers, product owners, solution designers and so on. Having this established, this is the community I am addressing, which I’m seeing as being a delicate group of humans passionate about technology.

All of us know how much effort is needed to bring an idea, a vision of a product into being and evolving it until it complies with all the requirements. We all know how much stress and pressure it implies sometimes. But let’s consider for a moment from where is that pressure coming? In a lot of cases, it is simply an artificial pressure coming from someone’s ambition to deliver as fast as possible, while in most cases that someone is not part of the developer community. We can understand that if, for example, a product gets to market before another one it might have better success rates, better outcomes, better profitability but all of that doesn’t justify the pressure when it simply might have just been a planning or strategy issue. There is no reason that the developers community must suffer in such cases, while in all of that the production of software is a creative process that should not be rushed.

Let us consider another aspect of how developers are somehow tricked out of their own rights. I think you all know about intellectual property and that whatever you advance, invent, patent in your creations belongs to the company you work for. 
How can this be right and how did we get fooled into accepting that? 
Of course, you probably made technological advancements, innovations while working on a specific topic driven by the company’s interests but it doesn’t seem fair that the company gets to take all the rights for those innovations, rather than having a shared benefit from that between the company and the developer(s) who used their own creativity for making it a reality.
Now let’s turn our attention to some statistics. Worldwide there are estimated to be around 27 million professional software developers but let’s be generous and add another 3 million and have a round number of 30 million developers community. The global population is currently estimated at 8.2 billion humans. Let’s say that not everyone benefits or uses software products and cut in half that number. We still get 4.1 billion humans that enjoy the benefits of software products. Comparing 30 million humans supporting, through software products, 4.1 billion humans I consider that the rate of service is very high and the rewards for such demanding jobs should be a lot higher than they currently are.

Consider also the profits that companies make with software products, which, in some cases, are in terms of billions of dollars while a senior developer earns around 120,000 $ per year. And this happens in countries like US where the income for software developers is considered to be quite good, while in other countries it is a lot lower than that.
All that stress, pressure and mental effort across the years drives a lot of developers to exhaustion, burn-down, depression and, in the end, a low quality of life.
So, I ask again: how is this fair in any way ?

So, the proposition and activity that we all need to do is organize ourselves in such a World Of Developers Union and start demanding our rights.
For this to have a real chance of success, it requires that all of the 30 million developers stand united and then the wheels will start turning. This will results in getting a real say at the table of negotiations for our rights. If all, or at least the majority of developers(~25 million), would stand united it would prove very easy to meet our demands. To illustrate this consider what would happen if all of that majority of developers would enter a strike of one month. 
How many software products releases would get delayed, how many promises/contracts would not be fulfilled by the companies, how many services would stop working affecting the whole world as we know it?
That is the power which can be brought to the discussions with the companies and encourage them to accept the requests we raise.

World Of Developers Union Manifesto
1.	The income of developers will have the following minimum ranges:
a.	Junior/Entry-level proving software development capability in a probation period of 3-6 months will have the minimum income of 100,000 $ per year worldwide
b.	Middle level development capability will have an income of 250,000 $ per year worldwide
c.	Senior level development capability will have an income of 500,000 $ per year worldwide
OR
Maintain current income ranges while providing a percentage (1-2%) from the profit of a software product to the developers that created that product. Of course, applicable worldwide.
2.	Reduce artificial stress through analysis of actual real pressure and, when signs of burnout appear, automatically offer immediate paid vacation (2-4 weeks) without decreasing from the normal vacation period.
In addition, offer mental/physical counseling and support services, paid by the company.
3.	All innovations, patents or technical advancements to be credited evenly (50%-50%) to the developer and the company while the profits from that to be split 90% to the company and 10% to the developer(s).
4.	Out of a working month 20% of the time to be allocated for the developers for own technological interests and advancements, projects, open-source contributions, etc.
5.	Evaluations performed for developers will be driven by developers, non-technical managers having only an assistance role. 
(It is strange when someone technically able is evaluated by someone non-technical.)

Of course, we can discuss further and adjust to reflect better our demands.
Let us unite and build a better world for all of us!
